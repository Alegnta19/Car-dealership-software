option.

* the authenticated `session_user` and effective `current_user` cannot conceal an owner

* owner URL plus `DATABASE_RUNTIME_ROLE=dealership_runtime` fails startup;
* the runtime login cannot assume any actual database, schema, migration, table, or

* it cannot INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, TRUNCATE, or otherwise alter the migration ledger;

reject/remove production startup-role switching.

supersession transition. In particular, `approved → denied` must refuse.
